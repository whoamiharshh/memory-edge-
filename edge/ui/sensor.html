Phone as a machine sensor

Put the phone on or right next to the running machine (e.g. 2 cm from a motor's bearing housing, or flat on a desk fan's base). Recommended: the microphone (44.1/48 kHz) - fast enough to hear bearing-defect impacts and shaft orders; measured on 20 real bearings with faults that developed naturally (0 false alarms in 380 healthy windows, 96 % of faulty windows flagged). The motion sensor is capped at about 60 Hz by browsers, so it only sees shaft-rate problems such as imbalance. Start the device with --profile acoustic for the microphone, --profile lowrate-accel for the motion sensor.
